This page uses a held-out Khan/STLD-style synthetic benchmark: two Brodatz textures plus one MPEG-7 foreground shape per image. The raw baseline is the union of frozen proposal-bank masks, and the ArchiTexture output is the learned single-region consolidation on top of the same bank. Zero-proposal cases are shown separately as proposal-bank misses, because their ArchiTexture panel comes from the no-proposal fallback path rather than a normal same-bank consolidation comparison.
